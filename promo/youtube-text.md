## Main video (promo-16x9.mp4)

### Title

Quiet Please - Turn off any song in Minecraft (mod showcase)

Other options:

- Quiet Please - Control every song in your Minecraft modpack
- Tired of the same Minecraft song? Quiet Please mod

### Description

```
Tired of hearing the same song again and again? Quiet Please lets you decide, song by song, what music plays in your Minecraft game or modpack.

In this video:
- Turn a song off and it stops right away
- Turn down just the one song that is too loud
- Play your own .ogg songs, right from the panel
- Build your own playlist
- Skip songs with a smooth crossfade
- Go back a song, or never hear it again
- See your listening history and stats

Download:
CurseForge: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/quiet-please
Modrinth: https://modrinth.com/mod/quiet-please
Website: https://mckycoskun1-commits.github.io/quiet-please/
Source code and bug reports: https://github.com/mckycoskun1-commits/quiet-please

Supported versions:
Minecraft 1.18.2, 1.19.2 and 1.20.1 on Fabric, Quilt and Forge
Minecraft 1.21.1, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 on Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge
Client-side only: it does not need to be on the server, so it works on any server.

Shown in the video: Quiet Please 2.0.1 on Minecraft 26.1.2 (Fabric) with Solas Shader, Iris and Sodium.
Music: the Minecraft soundtrack, plus three short songs made for this demo.

The mod's code and texts were made with Claude, an AI, under my direction. More about this on the mod pages.

```
