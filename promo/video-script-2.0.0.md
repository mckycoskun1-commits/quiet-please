# Quiet Please promo video (2.0.0)

Length: 45 to 55 seconds. Record once in 16:9 (YouTube), then crop to 9:16 for Shorts and TikTok.
No voiceover: short English captions carry the story, the game music is the soundtrack.
A reference cut of every scene is made by the recorder in `promo/recorder` (see its README); the real video can follow it shot by shot.

## Before recording

- Use a modpack world with many mods that bring their own music, so the panel list looks full.
- Bind the mod keys in Controls > Quiet Please: Open the music panel, Skip, Previous song, Never play the current song. They have no default key.
- Put two or three of your own .ogg songs in `config/quietplease/music` (Settings > Your music folder opens it). Only use songs you are allowed to publish.
- Play for a while first, so History & stats has real entries and play counts.
- Turn the HUD off (F1) for the opening and closing shots, turn it back on for the rest.
- GUI scale 3 or 4 so the text is readable on a phone.
- Record with the NVIDIA app (Alt+F9 start/stop) at 1080p 60 fps, every scene as its own clip and a few seconds longer than needed.

In 2.0.0 your own songs make no sound on Minecraft 1.20.1 and newer (the sound engine does not find the files), so the reference cut only shows the list in scene 4. Record the play click after the fix.

Note blocks and the modpack default preset are 2.0 features too, but they are hard to show in a few seconds; they go in the description.

## Editing

- Captions: white bold text with a dark outline, lower third, 2 to 3 seconds each. Put "New:" in yellow.
- Cut on the moment a song stops or starts, so the sound tells the story.
- End card: art/quietplease-icon.png on a blurred game shot.
- 9:16 version: keep the panel in the center of the crop; zoom in on the list in scenes 2, 4, 5 and 8.
- When the Modrinth page is approved, add "and Modrinth" to the end card.

## Music rights

The soundtrack in the clips is Minecraft and mod music. Mojang allows game footage in videos, but some official tracks are registered with Content ID, so YouTube may place a claim. A claim usually only moves the ad money and does not remove the video. To avoid it, use songs from mods whose music has a free license, or lower the music and add a free (CC0) track under it while editing. Your own songs in scene 4 must be songs you made or have the rights to.
