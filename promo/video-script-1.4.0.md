# Quiet Please promo video (1.4.0)

Length: 40 to 45 seconds. Record once in 16:9 (YouTube), then crop to 9:16 in CapCut for Shorts and TikTok.
No voiceover needed: short English captions carry the story, the game music is the soundtrack.

## Before recording

- Use a modpack world with many mods that bring their own music, so the panel list looks full.
- Bind the mod keys in Controls > Quiet Please: Open the music panel, Skip, Favorite, Pause. They have no default key.
- Turn the HUD off (F1) for the opening shot, turn it back on for the rest.
- GUI scale 3 or 4 so the text is readable on a phone.
- Record with the NVIDIA app (Alt+F9 start/stop) at 1080p 60 fps.
- Record every scene as its own clip and a few seconds longer than needed. Cutting is easier than re-recording.

## Editing in CapCut

- Captions: white bold text with a dark outline, lower third, 2 to 3 seconds each.
- Cut on the moment a song stops or starts, so the sound tells the story.
- Background of the end card: art/quietplease-icon.png on a blurred game screenshot.
- 9:16 version: keep the panel in the center of the crop; in scene 2 and 4 zoom in on the list.

## Music rights

The soundtrack in the clips is Minecraft and mod music. Mojang allows game footage in videos, but some official tracks are registered with Content ID, so YouTube may place a claim. A claim usually only moves the ad money and does not remove the video. To avoid it, record the scenes with songs from mods whose music has a free license, or lower the music and add a free (CC0) track under it in CapCut.
