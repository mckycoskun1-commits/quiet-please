# Voice lines for the 2.1 promo (ElevenLabs)

Story: a player enjoys a sunset with a calm, well-known Minecraft track, a terrible modpack song cuts in, the player complains, the narrator brings in Quiet Please, a fast feature tour follows, and the player ends relieved.

## Settings

- Model: Eleven v4 (tested 2026-11-18: it acts out the audio tags in square brackets and does not read them aloud; Eleven v3 also works).
- Stability: Natural. Player lines carry one light tag each and no "Ahh", "Man..." or sigh tags: the user found the sighing and breathing too much (2026-11-18).
- Two voices from the Voice Library:
  - Player: Chris (premade, "Charming, Down-to-Earth"), chosen by the user on 2026-11-18.
  - Narrator: Liam (premade, "Energetic, Social Media Creator"), chosen by the user on 2026-11-18.
- Make two or three takes of every line; v3 reads each take a little differently.
- Download as MP3 44.1 kHz 128 kbps (higher formats need the Creator plan).
- Save the files in `promo/voice/takes/` with the names below, adding `-a`, `-b`, `-c` per take (for example `p2-b.mp3`). The folder is not committed.

## Lines

| File | Voice | Scene | Text to paste |
|------|-------|-------|---------------|
| p1 | Player | A, calm sunset | Okay, this view is really nice. |
| p2 | Player | B, terrible song starts | Wait. Who put this song in my modpack? |
| n1 | Narrator | C, freeze frame | Ever had a song ruin the moment? [excited] Relax. Quiet Please fixes that. |
| n2 | Narrator | D, panel | Open the panel, find it, switch it off. [satisfied] Gone! |
| n3 | Narrator | D, volume slider | Too loud? Turn down just that one song. |
| n4 | Narrator | D, own music and playlist | Play your own music. Build your own playlist. |
| n5 | Narrator | D, skip, previous, never again | Skip it, go back, or never hear it again. |
| n6 | Narrator | D, history and stats | And see your history and stats. |
| p3 | Player | E, sunset again | [happy] Now THAT is more like it. |
| n7 | Narrator | F, end card | Quiet Please two point oh. Free on CurseForge. |

Target lengths: p1 under 3 s, p2 under 3.5 s, n1 under 4 s, n2 to n6 under 3 s each, p3 under 2.5 s, n7 under 3 s. If a take is longer, raise the speed slider a little (up to 1.15) rather than cutting words.
