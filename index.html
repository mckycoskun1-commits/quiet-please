Install in a minute

Quiet Please is a single jar file. It only goes on your own game.

  1. 1

    Get a mod loader

    Fabric, Forge or NeoForge, for the Minecraft version you play. Fabric also needs Fabric API.

  2. 2

    Download the right file

    Pick the file for your Minecraft version and loader on CurseForge.

  3. 3

    Drop it in the mods folder

    Put the jar in the mods folder of your game or modpack, then start Minecraft.

  4. 4

    Open the panel

    Press Esc and click Quiet Please in the top right corner.

Other ways to open it

  • Options and Options, Music & Sounds: the same button, top right
  • Mods, Quiet Please, Config (on Fabric this needs Mod Menu)
  • A key you choose under Options, Controls, Key Binds, Quiet Please. It starts unbound.
  • /quietplease gui in chat

Questions

Do servers need it?

No. It is client side only. Install it on your own game and join any server.

Does it work with modded music?

Yes. It lists vanilla music, music discs and music from other mods, for example the boss themes of L_Ender's Cataclysm and Mowzie's Mobs.

What happens when I switch a song off?

The game simply picks another song instead.

Does a song stay in the playlist after it plays?

No, it leaves the queue and the next song starts. Turn on its loop button to keep it: it moves to the end of the queue instead.

Does the playlist stop boss music?

No. Dragon, credits and boss music still play. The playlist only takes over the normal background music.

Why do I hear a preview with Music at 0%?

Previews play on the master channel, so you can check a song even when your music slider is all the way down.

Where are my settings saved?

In config/quietplease.json. Modpack makers can ship a ready file, and /quietplease export and import save and load presets.

Which languages are there?

English and Turkish. Other languages show the English text.

Can I skip a song with a key?

Yes. Open the panel and Skip the current song both have key binds. They start unbound, so pick your own keys.

Chat commands

Everything in the panel also works from chat. The commands run on your own game only.

CommandWhat it does
/quietpleaseMusic count for each mod. Click a mod name to list its songs.
/quietplease list <mod>The songs of one mod, with buttons to change them.
/quietplease volume <track> <0-100>Set the volume of one track.
/quietplease off <track>, on <track>Switch one track off or on.
/quietplease mod <mod> off, onSwitch every track of a mod off or on.
/quietplease reset <track>, resetallSet one track, or everything, back to default.
/quietplease gui, settings, playlistOpen the panel, the settings screen or the playlist.
/quietplease skipSkip the background song that is playing.
/quietplease export <name>, import <name>Save or load all your settings, playlist included, as a preset.
/quietplease dumpWrite the full track list to quietplease-tracks.txt.

What's new

1.2.0

Queue, loop and favorites

  • A song leaves the playlist queue after it plays, and the next one starts
  • Loop button on every song in the queue: a looped song moves to the end instead of leaving
  • The Repeat setting is gone, the loop button takes its place
  • Suggested songs next to the queue: your favorites, songs you added before and popular Minecraft songs
  • The star marks a favorite and adds the song to the queue. Favorites have their own group at the top of the panel, hidden while there are none
  • Shorter track tooltip. Hold Ctrl to see the full details
1.1.0

Playlist and settings

  • Background playlist with its own queue: play now, move, remove, shuffle and a progress bar
  • Skip button in the panel and /quietplease skip
  • Optional key binds to open the panel and skip the current song
  • Settings screen: menu buttons, now playing text, ambient default and the music gap
  • Presets with /quietplease export and import
  • Safer settings: an unreadable config is kept as quietplease.json.broken instead of being overwritten
1.0.0

First release

  • Volume and on or off for every music track, grouped by mod
  • In-game panel with search, mod filter and preview
  • Buttons in Options and Music & Sounds, plus /quietplease chat commands
  • English and Turkish
