Quiet Please

Too much music in your modpack? Turn any song down or off, one by one.

Download Try it below

Try it

  1. 1
    Press Play

    A song starts, played on note block sounds: harp, bell, flute and more.

  2. 2
    Drag a volume slider

    Turn the song down while it plays. All the way left mutes it.

  3. 3
    Switch a song Off

    The panel moves on to the next song that is still on.

  4. 4
    Star your favorites

    Starred songs go into the playlist below and play in your order.

These songs are short tunes made by this page, played on sounds modeled after the Minecraft note block instruments. In the game, the panel lists the real music from Minecraft and your mods. Your choices here are kept in this browser.

What it does

Everything happens in one panel inside the game. Your choices are saved and apply every time you play.

Every song in one list

All the music in your game, grouped by the mod it comes from, with search and a mod filter.

Turn off any song

Switch a song off and the game simply picks another one in its place.

Volume for each song

Set any song from 0 to 100 percent, on top of your normal music volume.

A whole mod at once

Turn off all music from one mod with a single click, and bring it back just as fast.

Listen first

Preview any song inside the panel. Background music pauses while you listen.

See what is playing

The current song shows in the panel and in the pause menu, so you can find it even without knowing its name.

Good to know

Only on your side

Install it in your own game. Servers do not need it, and you can join any server with it.

For modpack makers

Settings live in config/quietplease.json, so you can ship a pack with the music already tuned.

Works with big mods

Tested with the boss music of L_Ender's Cataclysm and Mowzie's Mobs.
